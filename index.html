📐 Cross Section Viewer

Click "Draw section line", click two points in the 3D view, then click Done in the top toolbar to finish the line. Then come back here and click "Use drawn line". Elevation is ignored — the cut is always a vertical plane through that line, so it shows everything crossing it at every height.
Alignment
Upload a CSV alignment, or pick one you've used before, then choose a chainage to generate a section perpendicular to it at that point.
CSV with a header row of station,x,y or just x,y (station is computed as distance along the line if omitted) — one point per row.
